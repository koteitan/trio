v0.118.156/record why multi-block induction plus (WL) is circular

- The largest residue branch (insertion inside the copied region) does close if
  the induction runs on the multi-block property: copy k's host column has level
  lev S p + 2*k*d1 and (WL) puts the lifted block at exactly that level
- But (WL) is derived from the core through (TOW), so the route is circular;
  (TOW) itself resists W_add because the last copy has the deepest root
