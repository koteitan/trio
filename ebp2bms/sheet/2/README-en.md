# Extended Buchholz psi <-> trio sequence, table: ε₀ ≤ α < Λ

[← Back](../../../README.md) | [Japanese](README.md) | [English](README-en.md) | [α < ε₀](../1/README-en.md) | [ε₀ ≤ α < Λ](README-en.md)

The correspondence between $`\psi_0(\Omega_\alpha)`$ and the standard forms of the trio
sequence system (the z<2 fragment), for $`\varepsilon_0 \le \alpha \lt \Lambda`$ ($`\Lambda`$ is the least $`\Omega`$ fixed point). Generated by `tools/build_omega_alpha.py` and
checked against every row of the BM4-Analysis sheet: of its 813 rows 706 agree
and 101 disagree (details in [dom.md](../../../dom.md)).

The general formula is on the [algorithm](../../algorithm/2/README-en.md) page.

**Notation**: the matrix is split into add units, and inside each one into anchor, root and
multiply units (digit plus embedding), shown by underbraces. $`\mathrm{B}`$ is the OT embedding.
