The correspondence between $`\psi_0(\Omega_\alpha)`$ and the standard forms of the trio
sequence system (the z<2 fragment), for $`\alpha \lt \varepsilon_0`$ (what the Cantor normal form of $`\omega`$ can write). Generated by `tools/build_omega_alpha.py` and
checked against every row of the BM4-Analysis sheet: of its 813 rows 623 agree
and 184 disagree (details in [dom.md](../../../dom.md)).

The general formula is on the [algorithm](../../algorithm/1/README-en.md) page.

**Notation**: the matrix is split into add units, and inside each one into anchor, root and
multiply units (digit plus embedding), shown by underbraces. $`\mathrm{P}(\gamma)`$ is the primitive-sequence embedding.
